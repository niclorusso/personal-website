1) Civilian casualties (OHCHR verified minimum)

OHCHR/HRMMU figures are verified civilian casualties, so they should be interpreted as minimums. The chart below plots killed and injured civilians for the months currently included in this release.

Caveat: verified minimums; true harm may be higher due to access and verification constraints.

2) Attacks on healthcare (WHO SSA incident counts)

WHO SSA records incidents affecting health care, with clear definitions and certainty tiers. This line chart shows monthly incident counts from the published Ukraine dataset.

Caveat: incident database, not a full census of all attacks.

3) Incident certainty profile (WHO SSA)

WHO SSA assigns each incident a certainty level. This chart shows how monthly counts split across confirmed, probable, and possible reports.

Caveat: certainty categories are methodological metadata, not severity scores.
