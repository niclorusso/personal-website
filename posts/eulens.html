EU Lens
← go back home 2 min read

I'm excited to share EU Lens, a platform I've been building to make European Parliament voting data more accessible and transparent.

The idea is simple: citizens should be able to easily see how their MEPs vote, discover patterns across political groups and countries, and understand what EU legislation actually means in plain language.

What you can do

The platform lets you browse EU Parliament bills with AI-generated summaries, explore an interactive hemicycle visualization of all 720 MEPs, and dive into individual MEP profiles showing their voting history and which colleagues they most agree with across party lines.

There's also a Voting Advice Application where you can answer questions based on real Parliament votes and see which MEPs align with your views, plus a PCA visualization that maps the entire Parliament's voting patterns into an explorable 2D/3D space.

Tech stack

The frontend is React with Vite, the backend runs on Node.js/Express with PostgreSQL, and voting data comes from the HowTheyVote.eu API. I used Claude (Opus 4.5) extensively during development to speed up the coding process, and Gemini 2.5 Pro powers the live AI features: bill summaries, the chat-with-MEP personas, and VAA question generation.

The whole thing updates weekly with new votes and automatically generates fresh summaries for any new legislation.

Why I built it

EU politics often feels distant and opaque. National media rarely covers what happens in Strasbourg, and when they do, the complexity of procedures makes it hard to follow. I wanted to build something that makes this democratic process more tangible, whether you're a policy wonk or just curious about how your representatives actually vote.

Check it out at eulens.eu and let me know what you think.
