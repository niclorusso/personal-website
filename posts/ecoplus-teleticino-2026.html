My appearance on TeleTicino

Oct. 30, 2026

On October 2 I was a guest on ECò Plus on TeleTicino, with Augusto Mitidieri and Nora Jardini Croci Torti, to discuss work, family and corporate welfare in Ticino. Thank you to Gianfranco De Santis for the invitation and for leading the discussion.

We talked about flexible work, working by objectives and young people who leave Ticino to study and work elsewhere, like me. It was a good discussion.

A few numbers: in 2022/23, 934 students from Ticino were enrolled at ETH Zurich, almost as many as at USI in Lugano (943). Five years after graduating, only 55–60% of those who studied outside Ticino live in the canton (DECS). The median salary is 5,708 francs a month in Ticino and 7,502 in the Zurich region (FSO, 2024).
