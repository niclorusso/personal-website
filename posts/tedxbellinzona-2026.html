My opening at TEDxBellinzona 2026

On September 12, more than 600 people came to the Cattedrale delle Officine FFS in Bellinzona for the fourth edition of TEDxBellinzona. The theme was Viaggio.

As Head of Curators, I shaped the program and worked closely with the speakers to bring their ideas to the stage. The speakers were Bruno Giussani, Vera Giampietro, Annalisa Manera, Roberto Balzaretti, Gea Cereghetti and Alessandro Mele. There was also an interview with Sergio P. Ermotti and Christa Rigozzi, and Caroline Roth moderated the evening.

TEDxBellinzona is organized by a team of volunteers, who give up evenings and weekends to make it happen.

I opened the evening. As I said on stage, I hoped everyone would go home a little more curious than they arrived.
