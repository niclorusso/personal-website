TEDxBellinzona 2026

Oct. 30, 2026

On September 12, more than 600 people joined us for the fourth edition of TEDxBellinzona. The theme was Viaggio.

As Head of Curators, I had the honour of shaping the program and working closely with our speakers to bring their ideas to the stage of the Cattedrale delle Officine FFS. A huge thank you and congratulations to Bruno Giussani, Vera Giampietro, Annalisa Manera, Roberto Balzaretti, Gea Cereghetti and Alessandro Mele for their enthusiasm and inspiring talks.

Special thanks also to Sergio P. Ermotti and Christa Rigozzi for a memorable interview, and to Caroline Roth for her brilliant moderation once again.

I couldn't be prouder of the TEDxBellinzona team of volunteers, always ready to give up evenings and weekends for organizing this.

As I said when opening the event, my hope is that everyone went home a little more curious than they arrived. Thank you to everyone who believed in us and helped make it happen.

See you in 2027.
