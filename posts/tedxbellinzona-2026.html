TEDxBellinzona 2026

A few personal notes on the fourth edition of TEDxBellinzona.
Oct. 30, 2026

On September 12 we held the fourth edition of TEDxBellinzona at the Officine of Bellinzona.

TEDxBellinzona is organized by a group of young volunteers. Within the group I take care of the creative side and the curation of the event. This year I also opened the evening.

The talk is in Italian.

More than 600 people came. Thanks to everyone who was there, and to the whole team.
