My talk at Hacking Democracy, ETH Zurich

On September 8 I gave a talk at Hacking Democracy: Co-Creating Innovative Tools for Participatory Politics, a hackathon organized by the Computational Social Science group at ETH Zurich.

I presented my research on the European Parliament, which is submitted to EPJ Data Science. With my co-authors I analysed 7,191 final roll-call votes from 2004 to 2026 with network science. The Parliament has moved from building broad and stable majorities to a fragmented chamber with rigid voting blocs.

The participants were ETH students from different fields, working on tools for participatory politics. We had good discussions.
