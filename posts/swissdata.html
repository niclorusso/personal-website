SwissData is now online

Feb. 6, 2026

I'm excited to announce that SwissData is now online.

  • Extensive dataset catalog: Browse through dozens of official Swiss statistics covering topics from population demographics to economic indicators
  • Interactive visualizations: Explore data through charts and graphs that make complex statistics accessible
  • Up-to-date information: Regular updates ensure you're working with the latest available data

Whether you're a researcher, journalist, student, or simply curious about Switzerland, SwissData provides a user-friendly gateway to official Swiss statistics.

Visit swissdata-platform.ch to start exploring.
